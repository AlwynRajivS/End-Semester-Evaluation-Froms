During Exam — foilcard & dummy entry

Pick a date and session, then key in QP code, foilcard range, dummy range and absentees. The tally turns green only when the dummy count matches the present count.

✏ Entry grid

Total Foilcard and Total Dummy are computed as you type. Present = Total students − Absentees. Duplicate dummy ranges are rejected on save.
📅Choose a date and session, then press Load courses.

Generate valuation bundles

Splits every course into bundles of the configured size, assigns a foilcard to each bundle and works out the dummy range it carries — then verifies the last bundle against the dummy number you entered.

✅ Pre-flight check

The Generate button unlocks only after the examination is confirmed over.
